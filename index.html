About the data

This page joins three public CMS files: Skilled Nursing Facility Enrollments, Skilled Nursing Facility All Owners, and Nursing Home Chain Performance. Each building is placed at the center of its ZIP code, nudged slightly so buildings in one ZIP do not stack, so a pin is close to its real location but not exact.

Star ratings are chain averages. CMS publishes overall, inspection, staffing and quality ratings per chain, not per building, in the files provided. A pin shows the average for the chain it belongs to. Buildings with no chain are shown in gray. The national average is 3.0.

  • Parent / chain is the chain or parent company CMS lists on the enrollment record. About 30% of buildings list none.
  • Owners 5%+ are the companies and people with a direct or indirect ownership interest of 5% or more, from the All Owners file. Intermediate holding companies appear next to the top-level owner.
  • Managers are management services companies listed with a managing or operational role. Only owners and managers with two or more buildings are included.

This is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CMS. Ownership data comes from CMS enrollment records and can lag real-world changes.
